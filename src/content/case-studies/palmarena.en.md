## The context

Palmarena Soccer Club has been welcoming players since 2015. It runs three businesses under one roof: a football academy for children and teenagers aged 4 to 15, 5-a-side and 7-a-side pitches for hire, and tournaments throughout the year.

These activities have different audiences and, above all, different questions. A parent wants ages, schedules, prices and a setting they can trust with their child. A group of friends wants to know whether a slot is free tonight. Both nearly always arrive through a search on their phone, and both leave if the answer is not visible within seconds.

So the club needed a site that gets found first, then routes every visitor to the right person, with no detour and no pointless form.

## The solution

The project started with research, not design. We studied what parents actually search for and the local pages already ranking for those searches; that work set the title and content of the Academy page, the most strategic page on the site.

The structure was then written down in a handoff spec: lowercase French URLs, one page per intent (academy, pitches, about, FAQ, contact) and permanent redirects from every address variant, so no link and no ranking signal gets lost.

The site is served as static files from Cloudflare, with self-hosted fonts: pages make no requests to any third-party service. The result is an instant display even on an average mobile connection, and no visitor data shared for no reason.

Booking runs through WhatsApp, because that is how families and players contact a club in Morocco. The academy and the pitches each have their own number, and every button opens a pre-written message — trial session, 5-a-side or 7-a-side slot. On phones, a call bar always stays within thumb's reach. Finally, an automated browser check at phone width verifies the menu, the call bar, the phone links and the images before every release.

## The club today

The site is live at palmarena.ma with six pages, each built around a single question: enrolling a child in the academy, booking a pitch, getting to know the club, finding an answer or getting in touch.

Every path ends in the same place: a pre-filled WhatsApp message, sent to the right person, with replies between 11 a.m. and 9 p.m.
